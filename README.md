## About

<img align="right" alt="AI Robot" width="260" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExcW50dWo2aHZhNHZ0eGxra2M3bGZsdXFucHB4b3RqeWt3ZGpjbHhiNiZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif"/>

I am a Bangladesh-based developer focused on **AI systems, chatbots, and automation platforms**. My work centers on one idea: complex AI should feel simple to the people who use it.

- **Currently building:** [sakibin.site](https://sakibin.site), a full bot management ecosystem
- **Focus:** making AI tooling accessible to developers and non-developers alike
- **Community:** growing the AI and chatbot ecosystem in Bangladesh
- **Open source:** maintaining Node.js-based Facebook Chat API libraries
